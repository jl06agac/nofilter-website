The truth is most of the coffee we drink comes from mega‑farms — land as far as the eye can track, ruthlessly efficient, mechanised and maximised for profit.

NoFilter · Field note 01 · On the cost of cheap

Cheapest means cleared. The most productive coffee comes off land the forest was pushed back to make — ranked, sprayed, mechanised, and planted to the horizon in a single crop.

It is a system built for yield, not for the ground it stands on. The forest that was there doesn't come back, and the species that lived in it don't either. The price on the shelf never counts it.
