What most coffee does

Grown where it’s cheapest.

The truth is most of the coffee we drink comes from vast monocultures — land as far as the eye can track, ruthlessly efficient, mechanised and engineered for the lowest possible cost. It floods the shelves and teaches us coffee should always be cheap, concentrating demand around the biggest producers while smaller, biodiverse landscapes struggle to compete.

At that scale, cheap stops being a price and becomes the expectation. Buyers gravitate towards the biggest estates, and the market bends around what they can produce.
