Why this coffee

Most coffee is grown where it’s cheapest.
Ours, where it matters most.

Every coffee we source grows in the agroforestry that hems a forest under pressure — Batang Toru, Leuser, the Arakan highlands — ripening under standing canopy rather than on the bare ground left where forest falls. Between 5 and 10% of every sale, ring-fenced before anyone takes a margin, goes straight to that work.
