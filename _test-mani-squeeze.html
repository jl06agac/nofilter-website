Why this coffee

Mostcoffee is grown
where it’s cheapest.
Ours, where it matters most.

And that’s the stuff most workplaces pour. Every bean we buy grows on the edge of a forest under pressure — Batang Toru, Leuser, the Arakan highlands — the exact line where standing forest meets cleared land. A proper price for coffee grown there gives the people on that edge a reason to keep the trees standing, and 5–10% of every sale goes straight to the partners holding that boundary.

Any supplier can put coffee in your kitchen. Most hand you a “sustainable” sticker and stop there. NoFilter isn’t built for that — and neither is the workplace that chooses it.
