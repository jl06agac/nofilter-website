What most coffee does

Fig.01

The truth is most
of the coffee we
drink comes from

vast mega-farms

Most coffee comes from vast mega-farms — grown where it’s cheapest, engineered for the lowest possible cost. What they squeeze out is the forested land where coffee still grows under canopy — land that only stays standing while the coffee finds a buyer.
