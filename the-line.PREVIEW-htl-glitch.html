Most coffee comes from vast mega-farms — grown where it’s cheapest, engineered for the lowest possible cost. What they squeeze out is the forested land where coffee still grows under canopy — land that only stays standing while the coffee finds a buyer.

2
The answer

But support can flow the other way

NoFilter backs the ones still standing.

We put demand back where it matters most — sourcing coffee from biodiverse agroforestry landscapes bordering threatened forests through NGO‑initiated projects. Every cup helps make those forests worth more standing than cleared, giving farmers and conservation partners another reason to protect them together.

5–10%of every NoFilter sale goes directly to conservation partners

We don’t buy from cleared land or offset after the fact. We back landscapes that are still intact, at the forest’s edge, through NGO‑initiated projects.

So demand itself becomes the reason they stay standing — making standing forest part of the value of every harvest.
