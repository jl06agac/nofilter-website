Layout A

Static split

The index.html treatment restored, with the film promoted into the right column instead of running full bleed. Type ranges left, stats sit under the copy in the same column so they read as evidence for the claim above them. Nothing moves. This is the baseline: if it does not work still, motion will not save it.

What most coffee does

Most coffee is grown
where it’s cheapest.

And cheapest means cleared. It comes off land the forest was pushed back to make, the edge between what stands and what’s felled slipping a little further every season. NoFilter is the other kind: grown on that standing edge, under canopy, so buying it holds the ground instead of taking it.

Between 5% and 10% of every sale goes straight to the NGO and conservation partners holding that boundary, a share of the price, on every cup, visible before you buy.

Layout B

Sticky media

The Obama Foundation mechanic. The film pins to the right while the type column scrolls past it in three beats, so one clip carries the whole argument rather than one sentence. Costs you nothing extra in assets and makes the five editorial clips go three times as far. Watch whether the pin feels considered or feels stuck.

Layout C

Broken grid

The Uncommon Studio move. The headline runs the full measure at display scale, then the grid breaks under it: copy narrow left, film wide right, baselines aligned at the bottom. Stats become a full-width band beneath, which turns them into a rule under the whole argument. Biggest headline of the three, so it is the loudest and the least flexible.
