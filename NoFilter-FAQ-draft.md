## For /wholesale-coffee

**What makes NoFilter's coffee "conservation coffee"?**

We buy through conservation projects run by named NGOs at each origin, from
growers farming the buffer zone at the edge of forest that is still standing. A
contracted share of every kilogram sold goes to the organisation holding that
forest. The share is written into the supply agreement and disclosed before
anything is signed — it isn't a marketing claim made afterwards.

**How much of what I pay actually reaches the NGO?**

It's set by the price you agree, in bands: five percent of the purchase value
where the coffee price is below SGD 46/kg, and ten percent at or above SGD
46/kg. If you choose to add a voluntary top-up, that amount passes to the NGO
partners in full, on top of the percentage. Contributions are calculated on
volume purchased and paid for, reconciled quarterly on 31 March, 30 June, 30
September and 31 December, and paid to the partners within sixty days of each
reconciliation date.

**Who are the NGO partners?**

PRCF Indonesia and SRI in North Sumatra, OIC at Gayo Lues in Aceh, and Fauna &
Flora Myanmar with ACCA in the Arakan Mountains. GJI joins the roll-call as a
partner not tied to a single origin.

**Do I have to buy the machines?**

No. Machines are supplied on a rental basis, and the equipment remains our
property (or our financing partner's) for the term. Purchase is available if
you prefer to own them. Either way we install and commission the equipment on
site, and we remain your sole point of contact for everything to do with the
equipment, service, coffee quality and consumables.

**What does servicing cover, and what does it cost?**

On a rental, bi-annual preventative maintenance and calibration are included,
and we bear the cost of all spare parts changes except where the fault is
caused by negligence. Response time for callouts is twenty-four hours. You
provide and apply daily cleaning materials at your own cost. On a purchase,
servicing is a plan you choose.

**How do I get a price?**

Build one yourself at nofilter.sg/trade-pricing. You choose your coffees,
machines, servicing and volume, and the wholesale rate, monthly cost and
conservation contribution update as you go. It takes about two minutes and
needs a work email — we send a code to open the builder. The figures it
produces are a starting point for a conversation, not a contract; what you
actually need gets settled by discussion and by what you find in practice.

## Also on /trade-pricing

**Is the quote binding?**

No. The builder gives you a considered estimate so you can see the shape of a
setup and what your conservation contribution would be at the price you pick.
Equipment rental is fixed for the term once agreed; consumables are fixed for
the first twelve months and then reviewed annually with thirty days' written
notice.

**How is coffee volume worked out?**

From cups a year, which is a rough approximation on purpose. Cup counts vary
enormously between a quiet office and a busy café with the same headcount, so
we treat your figure as an estimate for sizing the contribution, not as a
commitment. Real volume gets established by ordering.

## Per origin page (tune the partner and species to each)

**What does buying this coffee protect?**

Batang Toru is the only home of the Tapanuli orangutan, the rarest great ape in
the world. The coffee is shade-grown in the buffer at the forest's edge, and
farmers are paid to keep that edge where it is rather than push it back.

**How do I know the coffee actually comes from there?**

Every origin is mapped against official government conservation boundaries and
cross-checked with our NGO partners on the ground. Lots are GPS-located, so the
map rather than a batch number is the record. Three of the four evidence layers
are live today; the fourth is dimmed until it is.
