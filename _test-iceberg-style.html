What most coffee does

The truth is most of the coffee we drink comes from vast mega-farms

Most coffee comes from vast mega-farms — grown where it’s cheapest, engineered for the lowest possible cost. What they squeeze out is the forested land where coffee still grows under canopy — land that only stays standing while the coffee finds a buyer.

2

But support can flow the other way

NoFilter backs the ones still standing.

We put demand back where it matters most — sourcing from biodiverse agroforestry that borders threatened forests, so every cup helps make those forests worth more standing than cleared.
