Hold the line.

Conservation coffee from the forest edge

01What NoFilter is

There’s a line between farmland and forest. We pay people to hold it.

Coffee grown in the buffer zones on the edge of protected rainforest, where the pressure to clear is greatest. We pay farmers above the rate to keep the trees standing, put the monitoring in place to prove it, and return a share of every roast to the conservation partners on the ground.

02How it holds
i

Pay to hold the line

Farmers in the buffer earn more for coffee than for clearing. The incentive runs the right way, at last.

ii

Prove it with monitoring

GIS mapping and partners on the ground track the forest edge. A claim you can check, not a label you trust.

iii

Return it to origin

Ten per cent of gross profit goes back to conservation and farmer reinvestment, reconciled quarterly.
