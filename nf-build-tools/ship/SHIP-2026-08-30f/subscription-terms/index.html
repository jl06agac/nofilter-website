Coffee subscription

How your subscription works

Fresh coffee every 1 to 4 weeks, at 5% off, from conservation landscapes in Sumatra. Swap coffees, skip a delivery or cancel whenever you like.

Deliveries

  • You choose your coffees, bag sizes and how often: every week, 2 weeks, 3 weeks or 4 weeks.
  • We roast every Wednesday. Each delivery is dispatched shortly after the roast that follows its charge.
  • Delivery within Singapore is SGD 4 a delivery, free when a delivery's coffee comes to SGD 50 or more. If a change takes a delivery over or under SGD 50, the delivery charge follows automatically.

Payment

  • Your first delivery is charged when you subscribe. After that, each delivery is charged automatically, every 1 to 4 weeks as you chose, until you cancel.
  • Subscription prices are 5% below one-off prices. If you have a workplace code, you get whichever discount is larger, not both.
  • Payments are handled by Stripe. We never see or store your full card details.
  • If a payment fails, Stripe tries again and emails you. Deliveries resume once a payment goes through.

Swap, skip, change or cancel

  • On your order page you can swap coffees, change bag size or quantity, change how often, or skip the next delivery. Card, delivery address and cancelling are under "Card, address or cancel". Or write to hello@nofilter.sg.
  • A new frequency starts after the delivery already paid for. Nothing is charged when you make a change.
  • If you cancel, the delivery already paid for still comes; nothing more is charged after it.
  • Changes made before a delivery is charged apply to that delivery.
  • There is no minimum term and no cancellation fee.

Something wrong?

If a bag arrives damaged or isn't what you ordered, write to hello@nofilter.sg within 7 days with your order number and we'll put it right.

Your 10% to conservation

Every delivery sends 10% of what you pay for the coffee to the conservation partners in the landscape your coffee comes from.
