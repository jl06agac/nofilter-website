About

Coffee with
a boundary to hold.

NoFilter started with a simple refusal: coffee shouldn't cost a forest. We buy single origins from the communities inside Sumatra's most pressured landscapes, roast them for the workplace, and route a fixed share of every sale to the people holding the line on the ground.

No offsets bought elsewhere, no vague pledges. The coffee is good enough to stand on its own — the conservation is the point, made visible.
