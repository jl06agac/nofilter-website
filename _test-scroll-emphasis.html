What most coffee does

The truth is most of the coffee we drink comes from vast mega-farms

Land as far as the eye can track — ruthlessly efficient, mechanised and engineered for the lowest possible cost. It floods the shelves and teaches us coffee should always be cheap.

At that scale, cheap stops being a price and becomes the expectation. Buyers gravitate towards the biggest estates, and the market bends around what they can produce.

What gets squeezed out isn’t just smaller farms — it’s the diverse, forested landscapes where coffee still grows beneath a living canopy. Without buyers, those farms lose the income that makes keeping the forest standing worth it.
